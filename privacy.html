Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Application

NEVERSEEN Perforce Backup is an internal backup application operated by NEVER SEEN, an audiovisual production studio in Morocco.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive through Rclone to store and retrieve encrypted backups of internal Perforce version control data.

Data Processing

Backup files are encrypted locally before upload. The application accesses Google Drive for backup, verification and recovery operations.

Data Sharing

The application is intended for authorized internal use only. It does not sell personal information or share backup data with third parties for advertising.

Data Retention

Backup data is retained according to NEVER SEEN's internal backup policies and may be deleted when no longer required.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact NEVER SEEN through its designated professional contact address.
